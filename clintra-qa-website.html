About

Senior GCP QA expertise for sponsor-led trials

Clintra QA is a specialist GCP QA consultancy providing senior-level audit and quality assurance support to pharmaceutical, biotech and medical device sponsors. With over twelve years of experience across sponsor and CRO environments, the practice focuses exclusively on sponsor-led clinical trials.

Engagements are taken on selectively to ensure quality of delivery. Every piece of work is completed personally — there are no sub-contractors or junior team members assigned to your files.

The consultancy is built on the principle that QA should be practical, structured and actionable. Findings come with context, CAPA support and a clear path to closure.

Areas of Expertise
GCP QA TMF / eTMF Vendor Oversight Inspection Readiness MHRA / FDA / EMA SOP / QMS Risk-Based Monitoring CAPA Management Phase I–III Trials
Methodology

A structured approach
to every engagement

Clintra QA uses a defined, risk-based methodology across all service types. Sponsors receive a scoped, evidence-based engagement with clear deliverables from day one.

01
Scoping & Planning
Define objectives, regulatory context, study phase, vendor scope and agreed deliverables. All scope is confirmed in writing before work commences.
02
Risk Assessment
Critical processes and high-risk areas are identified and prioritised before fieldwork. Findings from previous audits or inspections are reviewed where available.
03
Audit & Review
Structured review against applicable GCP requirements (ICH E6 R2/R3, sponsor SOPs, regulatory guidance). Evidence is documented and cross-referenced throughout.
04
Reporting & CAPA
Clear findings report with severity classification, root cause guidance, draft CAPA plan and effectiveness check wording. Delivered within the agreed timeline.
Principles

What to expect from
every engagement

Scope confirmed before start

No work begins without a written, agreed scope. This protects your timeline and budget, and ensures the right areas are covered.

No sub-contractors

All work is completed personally. You receive the same senior consultant on every engagement, with full accountability for the deliverable.

Actionable findings only

Findings include regulatory basis, severity, root cause guidance and draft CAPA. Reports are written for sponsors, not auditors.

Selective by design

Capacity is controlled to maintain quality. Clintra QA does not over-commit. If the work cannot be done to standard, it is not accepted.

Case Examples

Anonymised examples from
recent engagements

All examples are anonymised. No sponsor, vendor or trial names are used. Examples are provided for context only.

TMF / eTMF

TMF Gap Analysis — Phase II Oncology Sponsor

A mid-sized sponsor preparing for a potential MHRA inspection identified significant gaps in their eTMF completeness. A structured audit was conducted across all critical trial documents covering three active sites.

Outcome
68 findings identified, classified and mapped to a CAPA tracker. TMF completeness improved from 61% to 94% prior to inspection window.
Vendor Qualification

Remote CRO Qualification — Early Phase Biotech

A biotech sponsor needed to qualify a new CRO prior to study start. A remote desktop audit was conducted against ICH E6 R2 requirements, covering monitoring, data management and trial master file procedures.

Outcome
Audit report and supplier risk summary delivered within agreed timeline. Three critical findings resolved prior to contract execution.
Inspection Readiness

FDA Inspection Preparation — Phase III Multi-Site Trial

A sponsor received an FDA inspection notification with six weeks notice. A rapid readiness gap assessment was completed across the sponsor team, two CROs and the TMF for a Phase III global trial.

Outcome
Evidence checklist and remediation plan completed within two weeks. Sponsor team completed mock inspection. No critical findings raised at inspection.
SOP / QMS

GCP QMS Build — Virtual Sponsor Start-Up

A virtual sponsor entering its first sponsored trial had no GCP QMS in place. A full SOP suite was developed from scratch covering all sponsor obligations under ICH E6 R2 and EMA GCP guidance.

Outcome
12 SOPs, 8 templates and a QMS framework delivered and approved within six weeks. Study commenced on schedule.
CAPA Support

Post-Inspection CAPA Management — Biotech Sponsor

Following a routine MHRA inspection, a sponsor received a formal action letter with multiple findings requiring CAPA responses within 30 days. Support was provided to classify, respond and draft the CAPA plan.

Outcome
All CAPA responses submitted within the required timeline. MHRA accepted the CAPA plan without further action at first review.
Document QC

Protocol & ICF Quality Review — Phase I First-in-Human

A sponsor required a GCP-aligned quality control review of a Phase I protocol and informed consent form ahead of submission. Review covered regulatory alignment, internal consistency and protocol logic.

Outcome
47 QC comments raised. Documents revised and resubmitted on schedule. No major queries raised by the ethics committee on first review.
